# Pre-event email (send 48h before)

**Subject:** Before Thursday's Clerk × Devin workshop: 10 minutes of setup

Hi {first_name},

In the workshop you'll turn an app idea of your own into a first version with sign-in, by prompting Devin, an AI software engineer, with Clerk handling authentication. You won't write code, but you'll need three accounts ready. Please set them up before you arrive, because Wi-Fi sign-ups on the day eat into build time.

1. **GitHub.** Any personal account works: https://github.com/signup
2. **Devin Pro, free with your code.** Sign up at https://app.devin.ai, then open https://app.devin.ai/settings/plans, choose **Pro**, and apply this coupon at checkout: `{coupon_code}`. Check that your plan page shows **Pro** afterwards.
3. **Connect GitHub to Devin.** In Devin, go to https://app.devin.ai/settings/connections/github, click **Add Connection** and pick your personal GitHub account. When GitHub asks which repositories to share, pick **All repositories**. Otherwise you'll need to add the workshop repo by hand on the day.

You don't need a Clerk account. Devin creates a temporary one for your app, and you can claim it afterwards if you want to keep it.

Bring a laptop with a modern browser and an app idea: a name and one sentence describing it (e.g. "Plant Pal: reminds you when to water each of your plants"). Nothing needs to be installed.

Walkthrough (optional preview): https://matheusdaros.github.io/devin-clerk/

See you there!
